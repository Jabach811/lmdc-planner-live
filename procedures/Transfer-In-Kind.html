Balance Import · Method 03 of 03

Transfer
in-kind.
Securities, not cash.

Assets move as themselves — same fund, same shares, re-registered to the new custodian. No liquidation, no market-day repricing. Buys runway, costs preparation: a three-week account-setup window comes before any wire moves.

§ 01 · Requires

The longest pre-work list of the three.

Re-registration is the core dependency. Funds keep their identity through the move, which means receiving accounts must exist, be funded with the right tickers, and be confirmed before any wire is booked.

§ 02 · Three-week runway

Submit, wait, confirm, then wire.

In-kind transfers are not a wire-day event — they are a three-week build that ends with a wire. The runway exists to give the receiving custodian time to open accounts that mirror the source positions exactly.

−3w
~3 weeks before

Submit to Matt O'Connell's team

Send the re-registration request package — fund list, CUSIPs, account roster — to the team that owns custodial setup. This is the hand-off that starts the clock.

−1w

Accounts ready & confirmed

Receiving accounts are opened with the right tickers/CUSIPs. The setup team writes back to confirm — explicit, in writing. No confirmation, no transfer.

§ 03 · Three phases

Prep, wire day, post-wire.

Phase · Prep

Set the table

  • Build Source Mapping from prior-RK test files
  • Build Fund Mapping from the signed TOA
  • Validate fund mapping in Informatica dev mode with QA
  • Submit re-registration package to Matt O'Connell's team
  • Build Informatica parameter file for the In-Kind workflow
  • Wait for account-ready confirmation in writing
Phase · Wire Day

Receive the shares

  • −3 days · advance heads-up to vendor & Cashiering
  • Day of · request final share file from prior RK
  • Confirm account-setup status one more time
  • Book the in-kind transfer · process incoming shares against the share tracker
Phase · Post-Wire

Distribute, reconcile

  • Update share tracker immediately on file receipt
  • Apply fund mapping a second time to participants
  • Reconcile share counts — every CUSIP, every line
  • Sign-off case once balances tie

§ 04b · Flow

The hard gate on the path.

In-kind has only one branching point that matters — the gate. Until accounts are confirmed open and correctly registered, nothing downstream can run. The flow is short, but the wait inside the gate is long.

§ 05 · Post-wire

Four steps from wire to participant.

①

Receive final share file

Confirm completeness against the wire breakdown. Update share tracker the moment the file lands.

②

Apply fund mapping

Second twin-apply pass — fund mapping fires again to land shares onto participant accounts.

③

Reconcile share counts

Every CUSIP, every position, every account. In-kind reconciles on shares — not dollars.

④

Sign-off

Final balance check, case closed. Share tracker filed as the system of record.

Hard gate · do not process until confirmed The receiving accounts must be confirmed open and correctly registered in writing by the setup team before any wire is booked. Process before confirmation and the shares can land in the wrong account, the wrong ticker, or fail re-registration entirely. This is the single most expensive mistake on the in-kind path.
Three-week submission rule Submit the re-registration package to Matt O'Connell's team approximately three weeks ahead of the intended transfer date. Earlier is fine — later compresses the runway and the setup team can't guarantee accounts will be ready.
Share tracker · update on receipt The moment the final share file lands, update the share-tracker spreadsheet. Don't batch the update with reconciliation, don't wait until end-of-day. The tracker is the source of truth that everyone downstream will reconcile against.
